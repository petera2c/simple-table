# No em dashes

Less —. Humans never use those.

Do not use em dashes (`—`) in copy, changelogs, blog posts, docs, UI strings, or chat. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses instead.

Hyphens in compound words are fine (`per-developer`, `pre-revenue`).

## Examples

Bad: dropped into React in minutes — backed by support that actually replies.

Good: dropped into React in minutes. Flat pricing, not per developer.

Bad: per product — not per developer

Good: per product, not per developer
